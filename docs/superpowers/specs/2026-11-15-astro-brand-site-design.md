# Blademainer Astro Brand Site

Approved direction: Blademainer is the umbrella brand. MacScreenCapture is the first featured product, not the permanent identity of the whole domain. Astro replaces Hexo.

## Experience

- `/`: product-led brand homepage, featuring MacScreenCapture with real application imagery and a working download path.
- `/apps/`: extensible product directory; no invented products or empty coming-soon tiles.
- `/apps/mac-screen-capture/`: product details, actual capabilities, installation, requirements, prerelease warning, downloads, source and support.
- `/blog/`: searchable articles with categories and tags; blog is separate from the product homepage.
- `/about/`: author and project context.
- Shared responsive navigation, footer, canonical URLs, metadata, sitemap, feed, 404, accessible mobile menu and keyboard interactions.

## Content and Compatibility

Preserve authored Markdown and static assets. Map old article URLs from the deployed repository, including timezone-shifted dates and aliases. Preserve category/tag/archive entry points and GitHub-hosted discussion links. Render formulas, code and PlantUML with proven libraries, without exposing former OAuth secrets. Drafts stay unpublished.

## Visual Direction

White, ink, vivid green accents, with restrained pale gray and blue secondary bands. Product photography is genuine software UI. The main experience is unframed and full-width, not a decorative card around a hero. Typography and spacing remain readable on phones. Avoid invented adoption counts, reviews, prices and performance promises.

## Delivery

Work in the existing `blog` checkout. Do not move the domain to another hosting provider. Preserve the existing Pages repository and custom domain. Validate locally before replacing the live homepage; provide a preview for review. No public deployment or domain changes before that review.

The user subsequently approved committing and publishing after checks pass. Install the reviewed Pages workflow in the existing deployment repository, retain the custom domain and game assets, and validate the real public site in the built-in browser.
